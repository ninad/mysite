Hi, I'm Mira — I build poetic interfaces and humane tooling.

I believe that the way text breathes on the screen says more than paragraphs of copy. My work lives where code hugs words and interaction feels like a well-tuned sentence.

About Me

Raised between letterpress studios and command lines, I'm obsessed with systems that feel personal. I lead projects end-to-end: research, interaction design, and front-end implementation, always grounded in story-first thinking.

My practice blends speculative design with practical shipping discipline. I love prototyping in browsers, iterating with writers, and carving out joyful micro-interactions.
